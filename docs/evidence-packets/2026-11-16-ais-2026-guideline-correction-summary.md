# Summary: published Correction to the 2026 AHA/ASA Acute Ischemic Stroke Guideline

**What this is:** a paraphrased, section-by-section summary (not a transcription) of the publisher's correction notice, written so reviewers can tell which guideline sections the correction changes. It is NOT a source for verbatim `quoted_text`. Verbatim text must come from the corrected guideline itself once the final PDF is held.

- **Notice:** "Correction to: 2026 Guideline for the Early Management of Patients With Acute Ischemic Stroke." Stroke 2026;57(8):e461-e467. DOI 10.1161/STR.0000000000000530. PMID 42507797. Published online 2026-07-27. Free access.
- **Read:** 2026-11-16, publisher page https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/STR.0000000000000530, in full (34 numbered items).
- **Parent guideline, final citation:** Stroke 2026;57(8):e316-e436, DOI 10.1161/STR.0000000000000513. The corrections are applied to the online version of the guideline.
- **Held copy status:** `Articles/Guidelines/2026-Guideline-for-acute-ischemic-stroke.pdf` is the FIRST PROOF (pagination e00-e00, downloaded 2026-03-17). It predates this correction and does not contain it.

## What the correction does NOT touch

**Section 4.7.3 (Posterior circulation stroke) is not mentioned in any of the 34 items.** Neither recommendation, neither COR/LOE cell, and no supportive text in 4.7.3 is changed. Section 4.7.2's recommendation text and grades are also unchanged (references and supportive text only).
