## 4. Newer evidence — HOPE (JAMA 2025)

HOPE, JAMA 2025;334(9):788-797, DOI 10.1001/jama.2025.12063, PMID 40773205. Retrieved via PubMed.
- **Alteplase** 0.9 mg/kg (max 90 mg), 4.5 to 24 h, n=372, 26 Chinese centres
- Enrolled **regardless of the presence of large vessel occlusion**; no initial EVT plan; perfusion-selected
- mRS 0-1 at 90 d: 40% vs 26%; adjusted RR 1.52 (95% CI 1.14-2.02), P=.004
- sICH 3.8% vs 0.51%; adjusted RR 7.34 (95% CI 1.54-34.84), P=.01. Mortality 11% both arms.

Bearing: independently contradicts BOTH narrowings in F1 (agent, and LVO/anterior). Addresses a gap the guideline itself names on the facing page ("The role of IVT in selected patients who do not harbor LVOs with >4.5 hours of symptom duration and salvageable ischemic penumbra requires further investigation"). Does NOT change the 2b grade.

## 5. Finding F2 — rendered Path C misattributes a trial restriction to the guideline

`src/pages/ExtendedIVTPathway.tsx:443`: "Current 2026 AHA/ASA late-window thrombolysis support is limited to ICA or MCA (M1/M2) occlusions, based on TRACE-III."
