## Source 1 — SCOPE IPD meta-analysis (Kent, JAMA 2021)
- **Resolves:** YES. DOI 10.1001/jama.2021.20956 → jamanetwork fullarticle/2787115. Full text verified; corroborated by Source 7.
- **Citation:** Kent DM, Saver JL, Kasner SE, et al. "Heterogeneity of Treatment Effects in an Analysis of Pooled Individual Patient Data From Randomized Trials of Device Closure of Patent Foramen Ovale After Stroke." JAMA. 2021;326(22):2277–2286. DOI 10.1001/jama.2021.20956. PMID not captured (retrievable, non-blocking).
- **Verified stats (match overview):** 6 RCTs; N=3,740; median follow-up 57 mo (IQR 24–64). Annualized recurrent stroke: closure 0.47% (95% CI 0.35–0.65) vs medical 1.09% (95% CI 0.88–1.36). Adjusted HR 0.41 (95% CI 0.28–0.60; P<.001).
- **CRITICAL — PASCAL derivation belongs HERE, not to the 2026 paper.** This 2021 paper derived the PASCAL classification (RoPE + high-risk morphology → unlikely/possible/probable) and the per-category HRs: Unlikely HR 1.14 (0.53–2.46); Possible HR 0.38 (0.22–0.65); **Probable HR 0.10 (0.03–0.35).** (RoPE itself: Kent, Neurology 2013.)
- **Stats framework:** IPD meta-analysis (forest-row). NNT NOT appropriate.
- **Confidence:** HIGH.

## Source 2 — PASCAL net-benefit analysis (Saver/Kent, JAMA Neurology 2026) — KEY PAPER
- **Resolves:** YES. DOI 10.1001/jamaneurol.2025.5446 → jamaneurology fullarticle/2844104. Corroborated by ESC/Tufts/press.
- **Citation:** Saver JL, Kent DM, Kasner SE, et al. "Patent Foramen Ovale Closure in Stroke and the PASCAL Classification System." JAMA Neurology. 2026;83(3):242–249. DOI 10.1001/jamaneurol.2025.5446. PMID not captured.
- **Verified:** Category distribution Probable 1,382 (37.0%); Possible 1,811 (48.4%); Unlikely 547 (14.6%) → probable+possible = 85.4% ("4 of 5"), unlikely 14.6% ("1 of 5"). Net-benefit (absolute stroke reduction vs late-AF increase): Probable strokes −2.5% vs late AF +1.3%; Possible −3.4% vs +1.1%; **Unlikely no stroke reduction (0.4%) + magnified AF +4.6% (net harm).**
- **CORRECTION vs overview:** This 2026 paper reports ABSOLUTE net-benefit differences, NOT hazard ratios. The "HR ~0.10 for probable" the overview placed here is from the 2021 SCOPE paper (Source 1). This paper's contribution is the net-benefit vs net-harm reframing.
- **Stats framework:** risk-stratified net-benefit re-analysis. NNT NOT appropriate.
- **Section 8:** 8a no dedicated paired editorial located (a related JAMA editorial PMC11646535 argues guidance lags PASCAL evidence). 8c secondary coverage says ESO now recommends PASCAL-guided selection for 18–60 — MUST be primary-source confirmed by medical-scientist before stated as fact; AHA/ASA 2021 Class 2a LOE B-R remains anchor. 8b/8d too recent.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for structure/framing/"4-of-5·1-of-5"; MEDIUM on exact absolute decimals (single journal-page fetch) — confirm decimals against full text before displaying as numbers.

## Source 4 — Danish nationwide cohort (Bonnesen, JACC 2024)
- **Resolves:** YES. DOI 10.1016/j.jacc.2024.07.015 (verified via PubMed).
- **Citation:** Bonnesen K, Korsholm K, Andersen A, Pedersen L, Simonsen CZ, Nielsen-Kudsk JE, Schmidt M. "Risk of Ischemic Stroke After Patent Foramen Ovale Closure." J Am Coll Cardiol. 2024;84(15):1424–1433. DOI 10.1016/j.jacc.2024.07.015. PMID 39357939.
- **Verified (abstract):** 1,162 PFO-closure patients (2008–2021) vs 11,620 matched comparators. 4-year absolute ischemic-stroke risk 2.5% (95% CI 1.5–4.0) vs 0.4% general population; 1-year 1.4% vs 0.1%; adjusted HR 6.3 (95% CI 3.1–12.6) vs general population. Authors: 4-year risk "comparable to clinical trials, but remained higher than in the general population."
- **CORRECTION / UNVERIFIED:** overview's "~0.47 per 100 person-years" is NOT confirmed by the abstract (which reports 4-y cumulative 2.5% + HR 6.3). Do NOT display 0.47/100 PY without full-text confirmation. Overview framing incomplete: omits the paper's headline that residual post-closure stroke risk stays ~6× background population.
- **Stats framework:** nationwide observational registry. No NNT.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for 2.5%@4y / HR 6.3 / N=1,162; LOW/UNVERIFIED for 0.47/100 PY.

## Source 5 — REDUCE MRI substudy (Messé, Stroke 2021)
- **Resolves:** YES. DOI 10.1161/STROKEAHA.121.034451 (verified via PubMed; AHA page 403).
- **Citation:** Messé SR, Erus G, Bilello M, et al. "Patent Foramen Ovale Closure Decreases the Incidence but Not the Size of New Brain Infarction on Magnetic Resonance Imaging: An Analysis of the REDUCE Trial." Stroke. 2021;52(11):3419–3426. DOI 10.1161/STROKEAHA.121.034451. PMID 34455822.
- **Verified (match overview + CI added):** N=560 (383 closure, 177 medical). Composite (recurrent clinical stroke OR new MRI infarct): closure 4.7% (18/383) vs medical 10.7% (19/177); RR 0.44 (95% CI 0.24–0.81), P=0.02. Silent/covert MRI infarcts: 3.4% vs 4.0%, P=0.81 (not different). Clinical strokes 1.3% vs 6.8%, P=0.001. No difference in number/volume/distribution of new infarcts.
- **Stats framework:** superiority substudy composite (bar-binary). NNT computable but NOT recommended (substudy composite, not parent primary); if shown, label as MRI-substudy composite.
- **Confidence:** HIGH.

## Source 6 — Biodegradable PFO device RCT (Zhang, Circulation 2026) — NEW TRIAL
- **Resolves:** YES. DOI 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.125.074609 (verified via PubMed + ACC Journal Scan; AHA page 403).
- **Citation:** Zhang F, Dong J, Wei P, et al. "Transcatheter Closure of Patent Foramen Ovale With a Novel Biodegradable Device: A Prospective, Multicenter, Randomized Controlled Clinical Trial." Circulation. 2026;153(2):71–81. DOI 10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.125.074609. PMID 41078120.
- **Year correction:** overview said 2025; issue is 2026;153(2) (online-ahead Oct 2025). NCT not captured — required before any dedicated trial page.
- **Verified (NI rules):** RCT noninferiority, N=190 (96 biodegradable / 94 nitinol), 7 China hospitals Feb–Jul 2021. NI margin −10% absolute. Primary = effective closure success at 6 mo: 90.63% (87/96) vs 91.49% (86/94); difference ≈ −0.86%; lower bound 95% CI = −8.98% (above −10% → NI MET). Device hyperechoic area gone on echo by 24 mo. Safety: no deaths, embolism, device thrombus, or erosion in either arm.
- **NI flags:** NNT NOT appropriate (block if proposed). Archetype ni-margin-chart (margin −10%, observed −0.86%, CI lower −8.98%). SURROGATE endpoint = echocardiographic closure success (device/technical), NOT powered for clinical stroke prevention; small N, short follow-up, single-country. Confirmatory of the device technique, not of stroke-prevention efficacy.
- **Section 8:** 8a accompanying editorial likely at Circulation 2026;153(2):82–85 — NOT retrieved (paywall); ACC Journal Scan frames device as "noninferior… necessitating larger long-term studies." 8b none (too recent). 8c no guideline incorporates a biodegradable PFO device. 8d none contradicting.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for NI result + safety. Gaps: NCT number + editorial content (non-blocking for a citation entry; required before a dedicated trial page).
